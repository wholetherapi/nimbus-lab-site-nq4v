---
title: Graph Priors for Sparse Rainfall Nowcasting
venue: NeurIPS 2025
status: published
authors: Nadia Farouk, Yuki Tanabe, Ana Ferreira
year: 2025
pdf: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12790
---

We embed a station-connectivity graph as a structural prior inside a
convolutional nowcasting backbone. The prior stabilises predictions in regions
where rain gauges are sparse and reduces median absolute error at lead times
beyond thirty minutes.
